# Methodology

## The Question
"When Social Capital publicly showcases a launch, how does that launch appear to move beyond the original announcement through secondary creator amplification?"

## The Constraints
1. **Zero Fabrication:** Only verifiable public numbers, names, and relationships are permitted in the dataset.
2. **Zero Duplication:** All entities (Cases, Posts, Sources) are assigned unique IDs and are relationally mapped. We do not count a YouTube video and a TikTok from the same creator as two distinct distribution relationships unless explicitly mapped as such.
3. **Fact vs. Analysis:** 
    - **Observed:** Directly visible text (e.g., "$100M ARR", "Sub-90ms latency").
    - **Interpreted:** Our classification of that text (e.g., "Institutional Status", "Workflow Utility").

## The Process
1. **Identify the Anchor:** We start with the public portfolio on Social Capital's website.
2. **Locate the Primary Post:** We trace the embedded X (Twitter) post by the founder. We capture the exact text, metric snapshot, and structural claim (e.g., valuation, tier-1 VCs).
3. **Trace the Secondary Network:** We execute public searches for the product across YouTube, TikTok, and tech blogs to identify how the broader creator ecosystem covered the launch.
4. **Compare the Messaging:** We analyze the delta between what the founder highlighted and what the creators highlighted.
5. **Extract the Finding:** We derive the non-obvious conclusion exclusively from the verified data, resulting in the "Distribution Bifurcation" insight.
